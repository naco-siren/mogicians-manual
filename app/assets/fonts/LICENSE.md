# MaterialDesignIcons-subset.ttf

A subset (eight glyphs) of the Material Design Icons webfont by
Pictogrammers, <https://pictogrammers.com/library/mdi/>, taken from
`materialdesignicons-webfont.ttf` version 7.0.96 as shipped in the
`material_design_icons_flutter` 7.0.7296 pub package.

The Pictogrammers Free License (<https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign/blob/master/LICENSE>)
distributes the icons and fonts under the Apache License, Version 2.0:
<https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0>

Glyphs included (code point → name): U+F00DC brightness-3, U+F00DD brightness-4,
U+F00DE brightness-5, U+F00E1 brightness-auto, U+F02A4 github, U+F02AA glasses,
U+F05E2 candle, U+F0825 guy-fawkes-mask.
